#### 12-Day Grand Discovery

*Formerly: 12-Day Bolivia Tour Package*

**Journey type:** Grand Discovery  
**Destinations:** La Paz · Lake Titicaca · Tiwanaku · Uyuni Salt Flats · Potosi · Sucre · Santa Cruz

**Hero subtitle**

Embark on an extraordinary journey across Bolivia’s most iconic landscapes and cultural treasures, from Andean highlands to tropical lowlands, in a perfectly curated 12-day luxury experience.

**A Grand Voyage Through Bolivia’s Living Heritage**

From the sky-high city of La Paz to the endless white horizon of the Uyuni Salt Flats, this immersive itinerary reveals Bolivia’s astonishing diversity. Discover ancient civilizations, colonial masterpieces, and vibrant modern cities while traveling through dramatic altiplano scenery, sacred lakes, and fertile eastern plains.

**Highlights**

- **Bolivia’s Icons, Seamlessly Connected** — Experience the country’s most celebrated destinations — La Paz, Lake Titicaca, Tiwanaku, Uyuni, Potosí, Sucre, and Santa Cruz — woven into a thoughtfully designed route that showcases Bolivia’s contrasts in culture, geography, and history.
- **Ancient Civilizations & Colonial Splendor** — Explore the enigmatic ruins of Tiwanaku, walk the silver-laden streets of Potosí, and admire Sucre’s refined colonial architecture, where Bolivia’s past unfolds through monumental sites and living traditions.
- **From Infinite Salt to Tropical Horizons** — Stand before the surreal immensity of the Uyuni Salt Flats, sail the sacred waters of Lake Titicaca, and conclude your journey in Santa Cruz, where lush landscapes and vibrant energy offer a striking finale to this grand discovery.

From Machu Picchu and the Sacred Valley to Lake Titicaca’s mythical Isla del Sol, the pre-Columbian ruins of Tiwanaku, and the surreal expanse of the Uyuni Salt Flats, this journey immerses you in iconic sites, rich traditions, and astonishing natural beauty.

- **Witness the Otherworldly Salar de Uyuni** — Venture into the world’s largest salt flat, where endless white horizons, mirror-like reflections, and stunning sunsets create one of the planet’s most surreal natural wonders.
